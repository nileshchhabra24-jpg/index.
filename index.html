Our Story

A warm corner on GT Road where every plate feels like home.

At Shree Shyam Vaishno Dhaba, we believe food should do more than fill a plate — it should remind you of your mother's kitchen. We cook only pure vegetarian meals, using fresh ingredients, slow-simmered gravies and a generous pinch of love. Whether you stop by for an early breakfast, a quick lunch or a late dinner — you'll find honest flavours and faces that remember yours.
